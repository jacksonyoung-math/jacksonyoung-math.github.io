ABOUT ME

Hello! I'm Jackson Young, a Mathematics Ph.D. student in my fourth year at Arizona State University. In 2023, I completed my B.S. in Mathematics at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in Troy, New York with a minor in computer science. That fall, I began studying at ASU, working under the supervision of Julien Paupert. My research generally concerns geometric structures, symmetric spaces, discrete groups, and arithmetic lattices. You can find a copy of my CV here. When I'm not doing math, I enjoy hiking, listening to classical music, cooking, and reading. (And, I'd be remiss not to mention spending time with my cat Leo).
